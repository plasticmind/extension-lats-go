LATS Go! settings

Work schedule

Choose the days you work, your times for each day, and where you usually work. Fill Timecard pre-selects these days, and you can change any day before you apply.

Week 1 is the first week of each pay period.

Week to edit

Partial-day leave

When you charge less than a full day of leave, LATS Go! adjusts your times so the day still adds up to your schedule. You can change this for any day in Fill Timecard.

Where partial-day leave goes

Moves Day In. For example, 1 hour of sick leave on an 8:30 AM day makes Day In 9:30 AM.

Moves Day Out. For example, 1 hour of leave on a 4:30 PM day makes Day Out 3:30 PM.
